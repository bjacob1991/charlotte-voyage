# Pg70–Pg99 review: fresh transcription (v2) vs the July draft

Prepared Nov 2026 to speed up proofreading. Read alongside `Pg70-Pg99-draft-v2.md` and the page images.

## At a glance

-   30 pages, 84 dated entries, Sat March 27 to Thurs Nov 11, 2004. The Nov 11 entry runs past page 99 into the next (not yet scanned) batch.
-   Every day name in v2, including the ones rebuilt from clipped edges, matches the 2004 calendar. The only mismatch is a slip in the log itself: "Wed June 14" written after "Tues June 15" (should be June 16).
-   `Scans/Scan 100-118.pdf` is the same scan as `Scan 70-99.pdf` (pixel-identical pages). There is no page 100+ material on disk yet.
-   Every even-numbered PDF page is scanned upside down with the left 1–5 letters clipped. That is where most brackets come from. It will also matter when you crop this batch.

## Problems in the July draft (fixed in v2)

**1. Four pages were summarized, not transcribed.** Pages 71, 72, 73 and 96 in the July draft are condensed retellings. Sentences are missing or reworded, and in one case the meaning flipped: page 96 says "an unimpressive crater"; the July draft says "impressive."

**2. Wrong day names on page 71.** The July draft has Mon April 14, Tues April 15, Wed April 16, Thur April 17. The log says Mon April 12, Thurs April 15, Fri April 16, Sat April 17, and those match the 2004 calendar.

**3. Page 80 (Bamaga) was reordered and condensed.**

## Place names checked

**Confirmed, spelled as in v2:** White Cliffs (Great Sandy Strait), Lady Musgrave, Pancake Creek, Great Keppel, Port Clinton, Scawfell Island, Goldsmith Island, Cid Harbour, Airlie Beach, Hook Island, Bird Island, Gloucester Passage, Palm Islands, Hinchinbrook, Mission Bay (Yarrabah, SW of Cape Grafton), Michaelmas Cay, Hope Islands, Cooktown, Lizard Island (Watsons Bay, Cook's Look), Howick Island, Stanley Island and the Flinders Group rock art, Cape Melville, Morris Island, Portland Roads, Margaret Bay / Cape Grenville, Bushy Islet, Albany Passage, York Island, Possession Island, Bamaga, Gove (the mine was Alcan Gove by 2004), English Company's Islands, Wessel Islands, Jensen Bay (Marchinbar Island), Port Essington, Alcaro Bay / Cape Don, Darwin, Ashmore Reef, Gunung Kawi, Ubud, Christmas Island, Cocos Keeling, Rodrigues, Port Louis, St Pierre (Réunion), Richards Bay, Tuzi Gazi marina, Hluhluwe.

**Small spelling notes:**

-   The log writes "Owens Channel"; the chart name is **Owen Channel** (Flinders Group). Leave the body as written; use "Owen Channel" for any stop name.
-   The log writes "Low Islands" and "Low Isle"; the chart name is **Low Isles**.
-   The June 20 "next pass through the Wessel Islands" is most likely the Gugari Rip ("Hole in the Wall"). The log doesn't name it.

**Not confirmed:** "Shallow Bay" in Albany Passage (no source found), and "High Peak" (probably High Peak Island in the Northumberland Isles; not checked).
