### [uncertain: Thurs] Sept 18 2003

Had a great time at the festival in Waterfall Bay. The winds were light while we were there, the weather hot & sunny, 15 boats showed up. Yelo came, we spent a lot of time with them. The 2nd day they prepared a traditional feast using all old methods, scraping coconut with clam shells, cooking prawns and [illegible] in pieces of hollow bamboo on hot stones. The last day we climbed to the top of the waterfall and heard the women making water music with their hands and arms. None of the white women could do it! ## LEG BOUNDARY — REVISED FINDING

The earlier estimate put the Vanuatu→Australia break at "around Nov 3–5". **The scans place it earlier and more precisely.**

The **Wed Oct 29** entry (pg62 → pg63) is the departure entry. It ends with the explicit farewell marker in the same style as the other leg transitions:

> "Goodbye Vanuatu — it has been a memorable two months!"

Under the project boundary rule in `plan.md` §8, the departure passage entry opens the new leg. So:

- **leg-06 (Fiji & Vanuatu)** closes with the last Vanuatu anchorage entry — **Thurs Oct 16** (pg61), the arrival back at Luganville.
- **leg-07 (Australia)** opens with **Wed Oct 29** (pg62), the departure from Luganville, and its first stop is the Vanuatu→Australia passage by way of Chesterfield Reef and Frederick Reef.

Note the passage is not direct: Charlotte routes Vanuatu → past Chesterfield Reef (Nov 4, not stopped at) → Frederick Reef (Nov 8) → Bundaberg. Both reefs sit in the Coral Sea between Vanuatu/New Caledonia and Queensland, so the whole run is one crossing and belongs in a single passage stop.
